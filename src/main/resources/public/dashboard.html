Bitcoin mining programmes

Choose the plan that fits your capital

Every plan is backed by our own Bitcoin mining operation. We run current-generation SHA-256 ASIC hardware in monitored, power-efficient facilities, sell the mined bitcoin at market, and allocate the net proceeds across participating accounts at the rate published for each plan. Hardware, energy and uptime are handled by our operations team; you choose the level of capital.

  • Owned SHA-256 ASIC fleet, replaced on a rolling hardware cycle
  • Sites chosen for low-cost power and redundant cooling
  • Round-the-clock monitoring, with mined proceeds accounted daily
  • Capital tracked per account, with statements available on request

The rate shown for each plan is the rate applied to capital placed under that plan. It is not a guarantee of future performance: mining output moves with network difficulty, hardware availability and the market price of bitcoin. Please read the plan terms before you deposit.
