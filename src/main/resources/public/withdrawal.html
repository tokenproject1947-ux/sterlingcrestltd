Withdrawal FAQ

Withdrawing Funds - How Does it Work?

At Sterling Crest Ltd, we have designed our withdrawal process to be as easy as our funding process. To begin the withdrawal process to be as easy as our funding. To begin the withdrawal process, first select your preferred withdrawal method and then type in the amount you want to withdraw and click "Proceed".

What methods are there for withdrawal of Funds?

Sterling Crest Ltd provides three(3) withdrawal methods (Bitcoin, Paypal and Direct Bank Transfer.)

Must Withdrawal Requests only be made at certain times?

Requests for withdrawals can be made at anytime via the Sterling Crest Ltd website. The requests will be processed immediately, and during the relevant financial institution's business hours.

Is there a withdrawal Limit?

Withdrawals are capped at the amount of funds that are currently in the account

How long does it take to get my money?

Withdrawal requests are addressed and handled as quickly as possible
